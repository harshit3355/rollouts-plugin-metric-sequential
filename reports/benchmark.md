## What this result does not establish

- The traffic is simulated. Real bad events are bursty and correlated (retries, shared dependencies, a bad node); the test assumes independent events at a constant rate within each arm.
- The test models bad events as Poisson. With Bernoulli traffic the conditional distribution differs at high bad-event rates; the 0.2 base-rate rows measure how much that matters here, not in general.
- Prometheus counts are extrapolated by `increase()` and arrive with scrape delay; the simulation uses exact counts.
- The threshold rule's setting (2x the baseline rate, failureLimit 2) is one common choice, not the best possible threshold.
- Nothing here shows the plugin running in a cluster; see the end-to-end test for that.
